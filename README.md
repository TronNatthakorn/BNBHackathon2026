# Tolly Easy Trade

**Find Market Movers. Trade Smarter. Every Day.**

## How to Use

1. **Export Raw Table Data (.csv)**  
   Export the raw table data as a `.csv` file once per day.

2. **Upload Raw Data (CSV)**  
   Upload your raw `.csv` file to Step 1. The system will process the data and generate a comparison file for Step 2.

3. **Upload Comparison Files**  
   Use the files generated in Step 1 and upload them to Step 2. Then, scroll down to the bottom of the page.

4. **Get Your Trading List!**  
   That's it! You will get a list of cryptocurrencies sorted by leverage in descending order.

   **Note:** By default, coins with higher maximum leverage are considered lower risk than coins with lower maximum leverage. This is a system-specific ranking assumption, not a guarantee of lower market risk.

5. **Check High-Risk Coins**  
   Coins with a risk value above 20 are generally not recommended for trading. However, they may still present potential trading opportunities. Always confirm with other technical analysis indicators before making a trading decision.

6. **Find Market Movers and Trade!**  
   Use the results to identify potential trading opportunities every day.

7. **Focus on Trading Signals**  
   You no longer need to analyze the charts of every coin individually. Simply focus on the coins for which the system generates trading signals to identify potential investment opportunities.
